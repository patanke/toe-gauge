How to use
  1. Level floor, tire pressures set, steering centered and strapped. Roll the machine back and forth a few feet to settle the suspension.
  2. Holder: phone upright, screen facing away from the machine, two or three feet that land on the rim lip (not the tire sidewall). Same holder, same contact points on both wheels.
  3. FRAME: hold the holder against a vertical surface that runs parallel to the centerline: a frame rail, a straightedge clamped to it, or a straightedge across a rear tire.
  4. Run FRAME → LEFT → FRAME → RIGHT → FRAME without setting the phone down in between. The repeat frame touches measure gyro drift and correct for it. Keep the whole loop under about a minute.
  5. No frame capture? LEFT then RIGHT alone still gives total toe.
  6. Adjusting: re-capture FRAME, seat the phone on the wheel, pick On LEFT or On RIGHT, and watch the live number while you turn the tie rod. Re-touch FRAME every minute or so; the live number drifts with time.
  7. Sign check: with the phone on the left wheel, steering right should read more IN. If it reads OUT, turn on "Reverse IN/OUT sign".
  8. LEFT = driver's left when seated. Toe inches = front-of-tire vs rear-of-tire difference at the tire diameter you entered.
